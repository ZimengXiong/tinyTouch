Review scope and writing style

This page includes unique CLI sentences and longer phrases with at least six words. Repeated wording appears once. Open “Used in … places” on an entry to see its other contexts. Short labels, command syntax, and diagnostic tokens are excluded from this review.

The proposed wording uses approximately 80% AES technical English: short sentences, direct instructions, consistent terms, and clear results and recovery steps. Commands, setting names, numeric limits, and template values keep their meaning. Prompts use sentence case. Error messages state the problem before the next action.

The left column shows the current CLI wording. The right column shows the suggested edit. Mark Reviewed to include a changed proposal in your feedback. Comments and your own edits are also included. Unreviewed suggestions are not sent unless you add a comment or edit them.

{value} marks a value filled in by the CLI. Keep template values unless you want to change the message content. Text fragments used after “to” stay lowercase so the complete prompt remains grammatical.

Edits on this page do not change the CLI. Drafts save in this browser. “Copy feedback” includes accepted proposals, edits, and comments across all filters. The complete original text catalog remains available as a reference.
